# Spec — Kiln & Clay   [FROZEN after stage 00]

## Client & business
Kiln & Clay — a small ceramics studio in Lisbon running weekend pottery classes for beginners. Differentiator: intimate, hands-on, beginner-safe (no experience needed), real studio with real kilns.

## Primary goal
Get a visitor to book a weekend beginner pottery class.

## Conversion goal
Click "Book a class" → complete the booking form (pick a date + details). This is what stage 04 optimizes for.

## Audience
Lisbon locals and visitors, mostly beginners, looking for a creative weekend activity (solo, date, friends, gift). Arrive curious but unsure if they're "artistic enough" — need reassurance it's beginner-friendly.

## Tone / brand voice
Warm, tactile, earthy, human, unhurried, encouraging. Must NOT feel: corporate, sterile, mass-market, or like a generic AI "artisan" template.

## Must-haves
- Hero with the value prop + primary CTA above the fold
- What a class is like (beginner reassurance)
- Schedule / upcoming weekend sessions
- Pricing (kept to ≤3 options)
- Booking form (date, name, email, party size)
- Studio location (Lisbon) + a bit of story/proof

## Off-limits / constraints
- Single-page site (one scroll).
- No fake reviews/metrics. Placeholder testimonials must be clearly flagged for the client to replace.
- Avoid the generic artisan cliché (cream/beige body, thin serif, stock hands-on-wheel only).

## Pages / scope
One page, single scroll. Sections: hero → what it's like → schedule → pricing → booking → studio/location → footer.

## Assumptions [assumed]
- [assumed] Price ~€65 per person for a 2.5h weekend session; placeholder until client confirms.
- [assumed] Classes Sat & Sun mornings; placeholder schedule.
- [assumed] Booking form posts to a placeholder endpoint (needs wiring by client).
- [assumed] Studio address is a placeholder Lisbon location.

## Foundation
Greenfield one-pager → single `index.html` + inline/linked CSS, no framework. Site files at project root.
