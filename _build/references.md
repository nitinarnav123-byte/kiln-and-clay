# References & Direction

## Register: brand (one line why)
Design IS the product — a studio's site sells the *feeling* of the space. Bar = distinctiveness.

## North-star references
- Award galleries (Awwwards/Godly/SiteInspire) for editorial studio sites — steal: large studio imagery, confident type, generous negative space.
- Workshop/class sites (Colorlib set) — steal: clean two-column class-info + booking split, streamlined enrollment.
- Student-work-as-proof pattern — steal: show real outcomes, not stock only.

## Anti-references (what to avoid)
- First-order cliché for this industry: warm cream/beige body + thin serif + single stock "hands on the wheel" photo. This is THE artisan-template tell. Banned.
- Second-order cliché: terracotta-everything + tiny tracked uppercase "HANDMADE" eyebrows on every section. Also banned.

## Scene sentence
A grey Lisbon Saturday morning; you step off the wet street into a dim studio lit by warm work-lamps, the kilns dark and heavy along one wall, cold clay going warm under your hands. → forces a DARK, warm-lit ground, not a bright airy cream page.

## Color strategy: committed
Deep fired-stoneware ground carries the page (~50%). Warmth comes from lamplight + glaze accent + imagery, NOT a beige body.
- `--bg`   oklch(0.22 0.02 60)   dark fired-clay/stoneware brown-grey (the kiln interior)
- `--surface` oklch(0.27 0.025 55) slightly lifted panel
- `--ink`  oklch(0.94 0.015 80)  warm off-white (lamplight on clay), used for body — high contrast on dark bg
- `--muted` oklch(0.72 0.02 75)  secondary warm grey
- `--accent` oklch(0.68 0.13 145) celadon glaze green (the classic glaze, used sparingly for CTAs/links)
- `--accent-2` oklch(0.55 0.16 35) oxblood/iron-red glaze, for one warm highlight
Contrast: ink on bg ≈ 12:1 (passes AA comfortably).

## Type direction
Contrast-axis pairing:
- Display: a warm high-contrast serif with character (Fraunces — has an organic, hand-cut feel; variable optical size). For h1–h3.
- Body: a clean humanist/grotesque sans (Inter) for readability.
Not two similar fonts. Display clamp max ≤ 6rem, letter-spacing ≥ -0.02em.

## Signature move
"Thrown, not stamped." Section dividers and the hero use a soft, hand-drawn irregular curve (an SVG thrown-rim silhouette) instead of straight rules — the page feels shaped by hand. The primary CTA has a subtle glaze-sheen on hover (tasteful, reduced-motion safe). One deliberate motif, not applied everywhere.

## Ship thresholds for this project
Technical ≥ 17/20, Design ≥ 32/40 (default bar; premium feel but a small studio one-pager).
