What a morning looks like

Two and a half hours, one lump of clay, zero expectations.

You'll sit down at a wheel with a small group, a cup of coffee, and someone who guides your hands the whole way through. You leave the mess to us.

01

We start slow

A quick demo, then straight onto the wheel. You'll centre your first ball of clay within ten minutes. Hands on, not watching slides.

02

You make two pieces

A cup and a small bowl, guided step by step. Wonky is welcome. Wonky is character.

03

We fire & glaze

Leave your pieces with us. We trim, glaze and kiln-fire them, and you collect them two weeks later.

The studio

A working studio, not a showroom.

Kiln & Clay is a small independent studio tucked into a quiet street in Lisbon. Real kilns, well-worn wheels, good light and good coffee. Between classes it's where our resident makers work, so you're learning in a room that actually smells of clay.
